## Year 1 Phonics Check practice

Practise the KS1 Phonics Screening Check at home, in the DfE format: 40 words (20 real, 20 alien), 4 per page, in two sections. Includes three set papers, a "New words" button for a fresh paper each time, marking, and a results breakdown.
